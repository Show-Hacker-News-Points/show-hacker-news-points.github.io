Y Show Hacker News Points

This site publishes every Hacker News story with 400 or more points, so the Reveal Top Hacker News Stories extension can tell you when the page you are reading was discussed without sending your browsing anywhere. The extension downloads the whole list and checks pages on your device.

The list is rebuilt from the Algolia Hacker News API every six hours:

  • feed-v2.json for extension 1.2 and later
  • feed.pb for extension 1.1 and earlier
