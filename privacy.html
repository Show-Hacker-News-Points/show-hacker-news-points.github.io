▲ Privacy policy

For the Reveal Top Hacker News Stories Chrome extension. Last updated October 25, 2026.

Short version: the extension doesn't collect anything about you, and it doesn't send the pages you visit anywhere.

What stays on your computer

The extension checks each page you open against a list of top Hacker News stories that it keeps in your browser. It stores these things locally, and nowhere else:

  • the story list itself, and when it was last downloaded
  • your settings: the minimum points, whether the grey dot is on, and any sites you muted (Chrome may sync these between your own devices if you have Chrome sync turned on)
  • a count of the pages you read this week that were on Hacker News, which the popup shows and which resets every Monday

Removing the extension deletes all of it.

What goes over the network

Once a day the extension downloads the story list from this website, which is hosted on GitHub Pages. Everyone downloads the same files. Like any web request, it tells GitHub your IP address, but nothing about which pages you read. GitHub's own privacy statement covers what they do with request logs.

A page address only leaves your browser when you click something that asks for it: search opens a Hacker News search on Algolia with that address, and submit opens Hacker News' submit page with it. Searching from the address bar only goes to Algolia if nothing in the local list matches and you press Enter.

What the extension doesn't do

No analytics, no tracking, no ads, no accounts, no selling or sharing data, and no code loaded from anywhere else.

Questions

Open an issue at github.com/Show-Hacker-News-Points/show-hacker-news-points.github.io. If this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it.
